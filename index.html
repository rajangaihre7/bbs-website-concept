About us

A family-run builders' merchant on Upper Elmers End Road since August 2010.

From a DIY shop to a builders' merchant

Beckenham Building Supplies Ltd started as a local DIY store and has grown into a specialist building supply centre, stocking bricks, blocks, sand, timber, tools, Calor Gas and much more, with a plumbing counter and computerised paint mixing.

Today we're a one-stop building materials centre with fast off-crane deliveries, open six days a week from 7am. We offer a personal and professional service to trade and DIY customers alike.

Between us we have more than 25 years of building and construction experience, so ask us anything about products and quantities.
